# What Is the Market Paying For?

## A Comparative Valuation Analysis of Microsoft, Adobe and Netflix

This project examines how the market prices different combinations of growth, profitability, cash generation and balance-sheet quality across Microsoft, Adobe and Netflix.

Rather than treating valuation multiples as standalone signals, the analysis places market valuation alongside the financial characteristics supporting those valuations.

## Market Snapshot

**9 September 2026**

| Metric | Microsoft | Adobe | Netflix |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P/E | 27.30x | 14.01x | 23.19x |
| EV / Revenue | 11.16x | 4.08x | 6.70x |
| EV / EBIT | 23.85x | 11.30x | 22.58x |
| P / FCF | 54.50x | 9.86x | 28.39x |
| FCF Yield | 1.83% | 10.15% | 3.52% |
| LTM Revenue Growth | 17.79% | 11.49% | 16.02% |
| Operating Margin | 46.78% | 36.07% | 29.68% |

## Key Findings

- **Microsoft** carries the highest valuation premium while also recording the strongest recent revenue growth, highest operating and net margins, and strongest balance-sheet position.
- **Adobe** trades at the lowest multiples in the peer set while producing the highest free cash flow margin and free cash flow yield.
- **Netflix** generally sits between Microsoft and Adobe on valuation while combining strong recent growth with lower operating profitability and a net debt position.

Lower multiples do not automatically indicate undervaluation, and higher multiples do not automatically indicate overvaluation.

## Methodology

Market capitalisation and enterprise value are measured on a common market date of **9 September 2026**.

The financial denominators are trailing figures based on each company's latest available reporting period. Because Microsoft, Adobe and Netflix have different fiscal calendars, their LTM period-end dates are not identical.

The analysis uses:

- **P/E** = Market Capitalisation / LTM Net Income
- **EV / Revenue** = Enterprise Value / LTM Revenue
- **EV / EBIT** = Enterprise Value / LTM Operating Income
- **P / FCF** = Market Capitalisation / LTM Free Cash Flow
- **FCF Yield** = LTM Free Cash Flow / Market Capitalisation

Free cash flow is defined as operating cash flow less capital expenditure.

No forward consensus estimates, broker price targets or peer-implied target prices are used.
